## Hi, I'm Subodh Patil

I'm a **Principal Information Security Architect** based in Stuttgart, Germany. I write about one question in many forms: **who is accountable, and for what, when an enterprise runs AI and data on infrastructure it doesn't own?** That covers AI risk management and governance, cloud AI trust boundaries, the EU AI Act and Cyber Resilience Act, encryption and key management, and the post-quantum transition.

I came to security from the inside of the code: I started as a software developer (.NET, C#, enterprise applications), spent time on Microsoft's ASP.NET / IIS / Azure Websites support team, and moved deliberately into security architecture around 2018. For the past several years I have led security architecture for a global, multi-tenant enterprise SaaS platform — cloud security, IAM, Zero Trust, compliance, and AI risk. That background is why these posts read like architecture reviews rather than policy summaries.

---

## What I Write About

- **AI Risk & Governance** — who owns and accepts AI risk; trust boundaries between cloud platforms and model providers; what ISO/IEC 42001, the NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act actually require of a deployer or provider.
- **Cloud Security & Key Management** — secure design on Azure and SaaS platforms; customer-managed keys, BYOK, BYOH and escrow models at scale.
- **Cryptography & TLS** — how HTTPS and TLS really work, what auditors check, and how to plan the post-quantum migration.
- **Compliance & Data Residency** — what multi-geo and data sovereignty mean for SaaS vendors and their customers.

Written for security architects, CISOs, engineering leaders and compliance practitioners who want substance over surface-level content.

## Editorial Policy

- **Independent.** This is a personal blog. No sponsorships, no affiliate links, no vendor-funded content. Opinions are my own and do not represent any employer, vendor or regulator.
- **Practitioner-sourced, non-attributable.** Lessons are drawn from real enterprise work across industries, deliberately generalised. No customer, incident or program is identified or implied.
- **Sourced and dated.** Posts cite primary sources (standards, regulations, vendor documentation) and carry a publication and last-modified date. Where a claim depends on a vendor's current architecture or a regulatory timeline, the post says so and I update it when the facts change.
- **AI assistance** {: #ai-assistance }. This blog reflects my own research, analysis, and opinions. AI tools, including Claude, may be used to assist with drafting, editing, formatting, and creating visual illustrations. Final content is reviewed and approved by me. Per EU AI Act Article 50, this disclosure ensures transparency about content creation methodology.
- **Corrections welcome.** If something is wrong or out of date, email me — I'd rather fix it than defend it. Material corrections are noted in the post.
- **Reuse.** Everything here is licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Quote it, cite it, build on it — with a link back.
