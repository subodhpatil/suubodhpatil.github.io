Hello all,

Everybody encounters some inexpressible moments. Offering you the Anniversary Issue of *Envision* is one of those moments for me.

When everybody around us was busy preparing for Ganapati Festival celebrations, the Envision team was filled with one thought: presenting you with a very dear gift in the form of this Anniversary Issue.

---
layout: page
permalink: /retrospective/editorial-september-2011-anniversary/

One has to struggle and work against obstacles to reach the desired height. But once you are at that height, the obstacles start working for you — the height itself begins to solve the problems. Envision was a journey filled with many ups and downs over the last year.

Throughout this year, we were experimenting. We published our first issue as a PDF, then moved to an online version, and later to a full-fledged blogging system. And today you are reading the first print copy. The journey from a 10-page *pre-release* PDF to a full-volume **60-page print issue** has been a learning, improving, and celebratory experience.

I promise you on behalf of the Envision team: with every upcoming issue, we will keep improving to build a great reading experience for you.

If you missed Envision over the last year, we have a *Best of Envision* compilation from all past issues.

---
layout: page
permalink: /retrospective/editorial-september-2011-anniversary/

I will not write much in this editorial — you have many wonderful things to relish in this issue.

But before I sign off, I want to sincerely thank the management of Decos for giving us the opportunity to publish this first print edition of the Anniversary Issue. And a **big thanks** to all Decosians who read every single article and provided us valuable feedback over the year — your input helped us improve with every issue.

I look forward to your continued support.

Regards,

*Subodh Patil*
